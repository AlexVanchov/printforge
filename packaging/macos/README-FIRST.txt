PrintForge for macOS
====================

1. Drag PrintForge into the Applications folder.
2. The first time, open it with a right-click (or Control-click) on the
   app > "Open" > "Open". macOS asks this once because the app is not
   from the App Store. (Alternatively: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
   "Open Anyway".)

Requires macOS 12 Monterey or newer, on Apple Silicon or Intel Macs.
Your projects are saved in Documents/PrintForge.
